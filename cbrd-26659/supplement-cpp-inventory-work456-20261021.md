## Reconciliation rule

A later add/migrate/remove decision must name the original assertions, their replacement, actual execution mode and a native passing receipt. An added product observer does not invalidate this retained inventory. Inputs with malformed stubs, out-of-range spans, interrupted operations, reused slots, latch misses or simulated hint state remain C++ responsibilities unless an equivalent reachable product seam is proved. The accepted SA prepared-row contract excludes unused padding byte identity; no case may restore that superseded oracle during deduplication.
