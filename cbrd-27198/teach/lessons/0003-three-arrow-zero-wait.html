Keep these three arrows in order.

  1. Requested: UNCONDITIONALThe disk manager says: “If the page is busy, I am willing to wait.”
  2. Rewritten: CONDITIONALpgbuf_fix sees the transaction's zero-wait policy and changes the caller's request: “Try once; do not wait.”
  3. Busy page: REJECT NOWThe latch cannot be granted immediately, so the conditional path returns failure without joining a wait queue.
UNCONDITIONAL → CONDITIONAL → IMMEDIATE FAILURE
There is no timed sleep anywhere in this chain.
